Hello! I'm Javier Porras-Valenzuela, a fourth-year PhD student in the Electrical and Systems Engineering (ESE) department at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Professor [Alejandro Ribeiro](https://alelab.seas.upenn.edu/). My research interests include: graph machine learning, constrained learning, optimization, and reasoning.


In a past life, I worked as an ML engineer for 7+ years, during which I applied machine learning and operation research methods to Supply Chain, Fraud Detection, and AdTech. 

I am currently looking for internship opportunities for Summer 2027!
